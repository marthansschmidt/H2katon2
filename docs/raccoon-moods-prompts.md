# Raccoon mood sprites

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool using `public/art/raccoon-atlas.webp` as an identity and style reference. Original generated images remain in the generated-images library. Project PNGs were resized with macOS `sips` to 512 × 512, preserving transparent alpha. `RaccoonCharacter` uses the full sprite for feedback and summaries and crops a portrait for the status card.

## concerned

Asset: `public/art/moods/raccoon-concerned.png`

```text
Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: ONE full-body character sprite for an existing cozy Estonian nutrition game. Input image: the raccoon atlas is an IDENTITY AND STYLE REFERENCE only; do not recreate its grid. Generate one standalone pose of EXACTLY the same cute gray raccoon with cream muzzle and belly, dark eye mask, amber eyes, striped bushy tail, yellow backpack and shoulder straps. Match its polished hand-painted pixel-art rendering, crisp dark outlines, fur texture, proportions, warm lighting. Square canvas, entire ears, paws, tail and backpack visible with 8% transparent padding. Head centered horizontally in the upper half, face readable at thumbnail size. True transparent alpha backdrop. No text, no symbols, no floating objects, no scenery, no frame, no checkerboard, no food, no extra characters. Preserve the raccoon's healthy body shape; express the mood ONLY through eyes, mouth, ears and posture. Supportive and child-friendly. Mood: slightly concerned and unsure, soft furrowed brows, wide questioning amber eyes, small closed downturned mouth, ears slightly lowered, standing with one paw thoughtfully touching its cheek and the other held near chest. A gentle worried expression, clearly different from the smiling reference, no tears.
```
