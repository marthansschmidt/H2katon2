# Roheline dinosauruse tegelane

Genereeritud sisseehitatud `image_gen` tööriistaga, läbipaistva taustaga.

Esialgne mänguvara: `public/art/dinosaur-atlas.png`. Mängus kasutatakse nüüd pesukaru stiili järgi uuendatud `public/art/dinosaur-pixel-atlas.png`; uuenduse prompt on failis [dinosaur-pixel-style-prompt.md](dinosaur-pixel-style-prompt.md).

Mängu avalehel saab valida pesukaru või dinosauruse. Läbipaistev 2172 × 724 px atlas sisaldab dinosauruse tervitavat, söövat ja tähistavat poosi. Valik salvestatakse mängu olekuga; varasemad salvestused säilitavad pesukaru.

## Prompt

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: a transparent game-character sprite atlas for an existing Estonian food adventure game.
Primary request: replace the raccoon mascot with an exceptionally cute GREEN baby DINOSAUR with VERY LARGE glossy expressive eyes.
Create ONE wide sprite atlas, with THREE complete full-body poses of the EXACT SAME dinosaur arranged left to right in three equally sized cells. Wide landscape canvas, ideally 1536 by 512 pixels. Keep each character completely inside its own third of the canvas with generous transparent padding, including the long rounded tail, hands, and feet; no parts may touch another cell. Same character proportions, same scale, same baseline in all three cells.
Character: adorable small bipedal green dinosaur, large rounded head, very big dark brown eyes with bright highlights, friendly smiling mouth, mint cream belly, tiny rounded dark green back spikes, short chubby arms, rounded feet and a soft curved tail. A small yellow adventure backpack retains the existing game's mascot motif.
LEFT CELL: welcoming hello pose, one small arm lifted to wave, facing the viewer, large eyes open.
MIDDLE CELL: happily eating, holding a bowl of colourful vegetables and a little spoon; same big open eyes and friendly smile.
RIGHT CELL: celebrating finishing the adventure, both small arms joyfully lifted, same huge open eyes and happy face.
Style: polished charming 2D illustrated game sprite, softly shaded chunky pixel-art feel with crisp dark navy outlines, warm highlights and rounded forms. Matches a colourful illustrated Tartu town game with cream UI panels.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely TRANSPARENT alpha background, no scenery, floor, coloured rectangle or checkerboard. No speech bubbles, no typography, no labels, no watermarks. Do not include any raccoon. Do not generate sad, tired, angry or scared facial variants.
